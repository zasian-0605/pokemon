# Pokémon Star Journey

This is an unofficial fan-made browser game project.

Pokémon names, game data, and related intellectual property belong to their respective rights holders.

Structured Pokémon data is accessed through PokéAPI. See the PokéAPI project and its repository for data/source information.

## Third-party pixel art

- Trainer walking and idle sprites, outdoor grass/tree tiles, and forest scene are from “16x16 Tileset (Pokemon/Zelda style:D)” by Damian Gasinski (Gassasin), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/16xx16-tileset-pokemonzelda-styled
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- The game scales the 16×16 sprites with nearest-neighbor pixel rendering. The PNGs in `assets/gassasin/` are unmodified source artwork except for cropping the atlas into individual reusable images.
